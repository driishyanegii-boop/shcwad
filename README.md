# LuxeStore - E-Commerce Website

A complete, fully responsive e-commerce website built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Pages

1. **index.html** - Home page with hero section, features, and featured products
2. **men.html** - Men's category page with all 12 products and sorting functionality
3. **product.html** - Individual product detail page with size/color selection
4. **cart.html** - Shopping cart with add/remove items and quantity control
5. **about.html** - About us page with company story and values
6. **privacy.html** - Privacy policy page
7. **terms.html** - Terms & conditions page

## Features

### Shopping Functionality
- ✅ Add to cart from product cards
- ✅ Add to cart from product detail pages
- ✅ Remove items from cart
- ✅ Update item quantities
- ✅ Cart persists with localStorage
- ✅ Real-time cart count in header
- ✅ Size and color selection
- ✅ Free shipping threshold ($100)

### Product Features
- ✅ 12 unique men's fashion products
- ✅ Product images from Pexels
- ✅ Star ratings and review counts
- ✅ Detailed product descriptions
- ✅ Multiple sizes and colors
- ✅ Related products on detail pages

### Design Features
- ✅ Modern, clean UI design
- ✅ Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- ✅ Smooth animations and transitions
- ✅ Mobile-friendly navigation
- ✅ Gradient hero sections
- ✅ Card-based layouts
- ✅ Professional color scheme (Blue/Indigo)

### Sorting & Filtering
- ✅ Sort by: Featured, Price (Low to High), Price (High to Low), Highest Rated

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Browse products on the home page or men's category page
3. Click on any product to view details
4. Select size/color and add to cart
5. View cart and adjust quantities
6. Cart data is saved in browser localStorage

## Technologies Used

- **HTML5** - Semantic markup
- **CSS3** - Modern styling with flexbox and grid
- **JavaScript (ES6+)** - Vanilla JS for all functionality
- **localStorage** - Persistent cart data
